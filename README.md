## Features

* **Full-Page Capture:** Automatically scrolls through the entire webpage and stitches all frames into a single master image.
* **Horizontal Slicing:** Automatically calculates and splits long pages into equal screen-sized parts (ideal for Figma or presentations).
* **Automatic ZIP Export:** Packages all sliced parts into a single `.zip` archive instantly.
* **Clean Capture:** Automatically hides cookie consent banners, dark backdrops, and floating chat widgets.
* **No Debugger Required:** Built on standard Chrome extension APIs (Manifest V3).

---

## Installation & Setup

1. Clone or download this repository to your local machine.
2. Open Google Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left corner and select the project folder.
5. **Screen Glimpse** is ready to use!

---

## How to Use

1. Click on the **Screen Glimpse** icon in your Chrome toolbar.
2. Choose your capture mode:
   * **Equal Parts:** Slices the page into equal viewports based on your screen height.
   * **Full Page:** Captures the entire page as a single continuous image.
3. Choose your preferred image format (`PNG`, `JPG`, or `WebP`).
4. Click **Capture & Slice** and wait a few seconds while it scrolls and packages your screenshots!
